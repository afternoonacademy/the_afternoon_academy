## Facebook — English

Homework taking over the evening?

Before adding more time, try asking:
“Can you tell me what you need to do, in your own words?”

Listen for the point where your child gets stuck. Is it understanding the question, choosing a method or knowing where to begin?

Our new parent guide, When your child struggles with homework, explores five patterns worth noticing, practical things to try at home and when to speak to school. It draws on UK education guidance and research, with sources linked in the article.

Read the full guide:
https://www.theafternoonacademy.com/guide/resources/homework-without-conflict

If your family would welcome extra support, our qualified teachers in Arturo Soria can help in small groups. The guide also explains how new families can request a free first group session, subject to a suitable place being available.

The Afternoon Academy · Calle Asura 40, Madrid
