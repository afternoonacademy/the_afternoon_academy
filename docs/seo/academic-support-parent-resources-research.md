# Academic support and parent resources — SEO research and editorial brief
Reviewed: 2026-10-19. Language: English and Spanish. Market: Arturo Soria / northeast Madrid.

## Evidence standard
- Primary sources: official government guidance, EEF research reviews, public education bodies, school websites for factual institutional information.
- Label UK/England evidence as UK/England; do not present it as Spanish legal or curriculum guidance.
- Link directly to the source in each published article; note date and limitations.
- Distinguish evidence synthesis from TAA's own services. Never claim an EEF average impact is an expected TAA result.
- Search phrases below are qualitative intent hypotheses, NOT measured keyword volumes, rankings or difficulty scores. Validate using Google Search Console once indexed, and Ads Keyword Planner or equivalent if available.

## Source register (direct links, publication context, editorial use)

1. Education Endowment Foundation, Homework, last reviewed August 2021. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/homework/ . EEF summarises +5 months average, +3 months primary, evidence rated low; task quality, curriculum alignment and feedback matter more than simply adding time. Not a TAA outcome claim.
2. Education Endowment Foundation, Metacognition and self-regulation, reviewed May 2025. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/metacognition-and-self-regulation . High evidence security; explicit planning, monitoring, evaluation, and teacher modelling; +8 months pooled average is not a guarantee.
3. Education Endowment Foundation, Reading comprehension strategies, reviewed October 2025. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/reading-comprehension-strategies . Comprehension distinct from decoding/fluency; modelling, inference, questioning; moderate evidence.
4. Education Endowment Foundation, Small group tuition. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/small-group-tuition . Targeting pupil needs, teacher preparation and linking to classwork; review applies to groups of 2–5, not automatically to any size TAA group.
5. Education Endowment Foundation, One to one tuition. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/one-to-one-tuition . Targeted, additional support tied to normal lessons; compare to small groups without implying one size always fits all.
6. Education Endowment Foundation, Parental engagement, reviewed May 2025. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/parental-engagement . School–parent support and communication, not pressure on parents to become teachers.
7. UK Department for Education, The reading framework, updated September 2023; applies to England. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-reading-framework-teaching-the-foundations-of-literacy . Guidance on phonics, fluency, comprehension and reading for pleasure; explain UK jurisdiction.
8. Education Endowment Foundation, Metacognition and Self-Regulated Learning guidance, second edition November 2025. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition . Practical strategies for teachers; adapt carefully for parents.
9. Comunidad de Madrid education: https://www.comunidad.madrid/servicios/educacion . Use the current official school admissions and transfer procedure, with year-specific validation, before giving deadlines or legal advice.

## Editorial template for each page
1. One concrete parent question and plain-language answer above the fold.
2. Explain who the advice is for (ages, curriculum, location), and when it may not apply.
3. Three to five evidence-backed insights with source links next to claims; show evidence limits.
4. Practical at-home checklist or questions for the school, useful without buying TAA.
5. When to speak with a teacher or qualified specialist rather than relying on generic tutoring.
6. Soft, accurate local TAA CTA, never an implication of school affiliation or guaranteed attainment gains.
7. Unique EN/ES title, description, H1, canonical, hreflang, FAQ only if genuine.
8. Internal links: parent resource -> related support page -> relevant school/neighbourhood guide.
9. Reviewed date, source register, annual review and immediate updates when government policy changes.
